Precision Arms – Short History Founded in 2020 by Adriel Macharia, Precision Arms is a modern weapons company based in Nairobi, Kenya. It specializes in high-quality, accurate rifles for military, police, and sport shooters. The company became known for its innovative designs, like the PA-15 Adaptive Combat Rifle, which works with different bullet types. By 2023, it started selling guns to security forces in Africa and other countries. Using advanced technology like 3D printing and smart scopes, Precision Arms keeps improving its guns. Today, it is growing fast and aims to be a top name in the firearms industry.
